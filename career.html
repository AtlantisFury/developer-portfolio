  4. Feb 2018 – Jan 2020

    Systems Analyst

    Shorefast Social Enterprises Inc (Fogo Island Inn) · Fogo Island, NL

    What I Learned & Developed

    After seeing my first real world operational impact of implementing a redesigned technical process that reflected the operational design of the business, I accepted a new role as Systems Analyst. In the following 8 years I went on to attain my Salesforce Certified Administrator designation and complete my Bachelor of Business Administration with a major in Accounting and a minor in Management. My Analyst role started with optimizing individual processes and systems, and evolved into managing Technical Services for all of Shorefast's charitable programs and social businesses. This has afforded me the learning opportunities of how to navigate non-profit system administration, vendor management, contract negotiation, collaborate on custom support agreements for remote locations, build redundancy into utilities, building systems, software, and my favourite endeavour of building business process automation. My experience has built specializations in end user tech support for Windows environments, technical services strategic planning, hardware repair and lifecycle management, CRM solutions for non-profit/fundraising and world class hospitality solutions, hotel property management solutions, IT project management, non-profit and hospitality accounting and operational reporting. All of this through the unique lens of working for a Canadian charity and social enterprise with the challenges of being in a remote rural area.

  6. May 2015 – Sept 2015

    Group Sales Intern

    Terra Nova Resort & Golf Community · Port Blandford, NL

    What I Learned & Developed

    As a Group Sales Intern at Terra Nova Golf Resort, I gained hands-on experience across the full hospitality sales cycle, from the first lead inquiry through contract signature to event delivery. I worked directly with the group sales manager, operations manager, front desk, maintenance, bar, and pro-shop teams, which gave me a well-rounded view of how a resort runs day to day. I became proficient in the property management system, handling reservations, upselling golf packages, and learning how availability, overbooking rules, and group block management shape booking decisions for an 85 room property. On the front desk, I was trained in guest complaint handling and service recovery, including walking overbooked reservations and resolving maintenance and housekeeping issues. I also learned to tailor my approach to different guest types, from bus tours to weddings and conferences. The role built my skills in customer communication, problem-solving, and cross-team collaboration
